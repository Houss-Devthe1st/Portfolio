"Ya Zalame Drive!"

Unity · Android · Google AdMob

Available on Play Store

An endless car game set on the chaotic Lebanese roads: Dodge obstacles, collect coins, and survive as long as you can. Built in Unity with C#, the game features multiple difficulty levels, an in-game car shop, achievements, a non-copyrighted radio system, and monetisation through Google AdMob and Unity Ads.
