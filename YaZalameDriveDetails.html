Ya Zalame Drive!

An endless driving game for Android, set on the chaotic roads of Lebanon.

Type
Mobile game
Built with
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Android
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
Status
Available on Google Play

Overview

Dodge obstacles, collect coins and survive as long as you can. I built the game in Unity with C#. It has multiple difficulty levels, an in-game car shop, achievements and a radio with copyright-free music, and it earns revenue through Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

Difficulty levels

Several levels, each harder than the last.

Achievements

Goals to unlock as you play.

Radio

An in-game radio that plays copyright-free music.

Car shop

Collect coins on the road and spend them on new cars.

Score tracking

Keeps your high score and your last score.

Ads

Google AdMob and Unity Ads are both integrated.
